About me

I came to security from networking, so I tend to start with how traffic moves: what's listening, what talks to what, and where trust is assumed. My goal is an offensive security role, penetration testing first and red teaming later, and I'm also open to entry-level IT roles as a way in.

My foundations are hands-on. A Certificate II in Computer Assembly & Repair taught me to build, upgrade and fault-find PCs, and my Certificate IV in Information Technology added client troubleshooting, introductory programming, cyber security risk management, and IT ethics and privacy. Cisco's Introduction to Cyber Security and TryHackMe's Pre Security path then took me into threats, attacks and how networks and the web work underneath.

Away from the keyboard I supervise a food and beverage team at Crown Melbourne, backed by a Certificate IV in Leadership & Management. That job taught me to stay calm under pressure, follow strict compliance rules, write clear incident notes and lead a team on long night shifts, all of which carry straight into client work.
