03reports

Penetration test reports

Each engagement follows the same phases a consultancy would run, and ends in a client-style report with an executive summary, risk-rated findings and remediation.

Completed · v1.0REPORT 001

Metasploitable 2: vsftpd 2.3.4 backdoor to root (port 21)

A single-day engagement against the FTP service on Metasploitable 2. Nmap identified vsftpd 2.3.4, a build that was tampered with before release. After reading the public exploit to understand the trigger, I fired the backdoor by hand with an FTP login ending in :) and connected to the hidden shell on port 6200 with Netcat, getting an unauthenticated root shell. I then reproduced the result in Metasploit and wrote it up with CVSS scoring, reproduction steps, impact, remediation and retest notes.
